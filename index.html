Technogym treadmill — how to set it up
⚠️
Default gradient starts at 1% — always raise it first. At 1% you burn roughly half the calories vs 6% at the same speed. Tap GRADIENT + five times before starting your walk.
TECHNOGYM SCREEN CONTROLS
GRADIENT (left side)
Tap + next to GRADIENT at bottom left. Each tap = +1%. Quick-select buttons on left panel: 0, 3, 6, 9. Tap 6 directly for Week 1 target.
SPEED (right side)
Tap +/− next to SPEED at bottom right. Quick buttons: 4, 6, 8, 10 km/h. Set to 4.5 using + from 4.
HR MONITOR
Shows BPM top right. Grip the metal handles on side rails to read. Him target: 102–111 bpm. Her: 104–113 bpm.
LOG IN (top right)

🏊 Pool protocol — add to any session
14m heated indoor pool · Harbour Bridge views · burns 400–600 kcal/hr
Beginner lane swim (Weeks 1–4)
4 laps easy freestyle → 2 laps backstroke → rest 60s → repeat 3 sets. ~20 min total. Low joint impact — great on leg days.
Interval swim (Month 2+)
2 laps fast → 1 lap easy recovery → repeat 8 rounds. ~25 min. Excellent cardio without treadmill load on joints.
Active recovery swim (Sat or rest days)
10–15 min easy float and stretch. Increases blood flow to worked muscles without adding fatigue load.
🔄 Recovery protocol — post-workout ritual
Science-backed contrast therapy. Especially powerful on heavy leg days (Tue/Thu) and after Fri full body.
1
Dry sauna — 10–12 min
Raises core temp, flushes lactic acid, boosts growth hormone release. Sit quietly, breathe deeply.
2
Cold plunge — 2–3 min
Constricts blood vessels, reduces inflammation and DOMS. Breathe slowly. Build to 3 min over Weeks 1–2.
3
Repeat 2–3 cycles
Sauna → cold plunge → sauna → cold plunge. Each cycle drives blood in and out of muscles — the "pump" that accelerates recovery.
4
Vitality/spa pool — 8–10 min
End here. Jets target lower back and legs. Parasympathetic nervous system activation — signals full recovery mode.
5
Steam room — optional 5 min
Moist heat opens airways, clears sinuses, promotes deep skin hydration. Good for immunity support. Drink water before and after.
💧 Hydration rule: Drink 500ml water immediately post-gym before entering sauna. Add pinch of pink Himalayan salt. This is critical — sauna + training = major fluid loss.
